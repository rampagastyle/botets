Privacy Policy

VideoProcessing (TikTok app name: rampaga) · Last updated: 2026-09-29

This Privacy Policy explains what data the VideoProcessing Telegram bot (“we”, “service”) processes when you use the service and optional TikTok integration.

1. Data we process

  • Telegram: user id, username (if available), messages and media you send to the bot.
  • Processing files: temporary video/audio files needed to cut and export clips; deleted after processing when possible.
  • Settings: preferences such as clip length, quality, selected banner, language.
  • TikTok (optional): OAuth tokens and basic profile identifiers required to upload content on your behalf after you authorize the app.
  • Technical logs: errors and operational logs for stability and security.

2. Purpose

  • Provide video cutting, banner overlay, and text suggestions.
  • Authenticate and upload to TikTok only when you request it.
  • Maintain allowlist/admin controls and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting) and by TikTok when you use TikTok login/upload, under TikTok’s policies.

4. Retention

  • Temporary media files are removed after jobs complete or during cleanup.
  • Settings and allowlist entries are kept while you use the service.
  • TikTok tokens are stored only to perform requested uploads and can be removed when you disconnect or ask support.

5. Your choices

  • Stop using the bot and request deletion of stored settings/tokens via support.
  • Revoke TikTok access in TikTok application settings.

6. Contact

Privacy requests: @classismfact on Telegram.
